TAKE THE SCENIC ROUTE
Your Tokyo test drive.
Explore a small, real OpenStreetMap neighborhood in a low-poly car. Find three orange checkpoints, or just drive.
  • W / ↑ Accelerate
  • S / ↓ Brake, then reverse
  • A D / ← → Steer
  • Space Handbrake · Hold Shift Nitro boost
  • C Change camera · R Reset car
  • M Mute/unmute engine, nitro and tyre sounds
  • I Toggle ignition (starts/stops trips) · P Pause driving
Start the local app with npm start to send native Teltonika TCP. Enter your receiver host, port and allowed IMEI, then connect. Delivery is confirmed only after your server acknowledges the record.
Autonomous trip: choose a distance below the game, optionally select events, then start. It follows connected roads and ends with the engine off. Use Pause to hold the run or Stop trip to finish early. Driving keys take over manually.
Device timing separates collection and upload intervals for engine on/off. Choose Fast test for 12× simulation: 5 real minutes generates one hour. Timestamps, collection and uploads use this faster clock. Manual driving and the camera stay at normal speed; autonomous trips accelerate with the clock. The dashboard shows driving speed. Manual AVL speed and acceleration are adjusted to match GPS movement over simulated time, so manual AVL speeds are lower at higher multipliers. Ignition/events are collected immediately, then uploaded in batches. Send now flushes pending data. Pause or hide the tab to hold the clock. JSON export includes collected points and timing settings. Synthetic altitude and satellite values remain fixed.
