TAKE THE SCENIC ROUTE
Your Tokyo test drive.
Explore a small, real OpenStreetMap neighborhood in a low-poly car. Find three orange checkpoints, or just drive.
  • W / ↑ Accelerate
  • S / ↓ Brake, then reverse
  • A D / ← → Steer
  • Space Handbrake · Hold Shift Nitro boost
  • C Change camera · R Reset car
  • I Toggle ignition (starts/stops trips) · P Pause driving
Start the local app with npm start to send native Teltonika TCP. Enter your receiver host, port and allowed IMEI, then connect. Delivery is confirmed only after your server acknowledges the record.
GPS points are recorded at your chosen interval and can be exported as JSON. Synthetic altitude and satellite values remain fixed.
